Your data, explained

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 30, 2026

HluWeatherApp is an Android weather app maintained by homoludens. This policy describes the app and this website. For questions, contact playstore@droopia.net.

The app does not require an account or send your weather requests to a server operated by the developer. It does send information directly to the services needed to provide forecasts, maps, place search, and routes, as described below.

Information the app uses

  • Locations. Places you select or save can include a name, latitude, longitude, and altitude. If you grant Android location permission and use a location feature such as Track Me, the app can access your approximate or precise device location, depending on the permission you grant.
  • Searches and routes. Place searches and the start and destination you choose for a trip are used to find places, calculate a route, and request weather along it.
  • Settings and cached data. The app saves preferences, saved locations, a last-known device location, forecast data, and information used for widgets and notifications on your device.

Location permission is optional. You can choose saved places without granting access to your device location. Track Me obtains location updates while the app is in use; widgets may use a saved or last-known location for background weather updates. Enabled notifications can also trigger background forecast requests for a saved location.

Information sent to other services

To provide its features, the app sends requests directly from your device to the following services. Which requests occur depends on the features and providers you use:

  • Open-Meteo and MET Norway: coordinates and, where applicable, altitude for weather forecasts. Open-Meteo also provides air-quality forecasts, place search, and forecasts for points along a trip.
  • Photon (Komoot) or Open-Meteo geocoding: the place-search text you enter, according to your selected search provider.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): coordinates to look up a place name.
  • OSRM: the start and destination coordinates for a driving route.
  • OpenFreeMap: map styles and tiles; requests for map tiles can reveal the area of the map being viewed.
  • Google Play services: the Google version of the app uses Google's fused location provider when a device-location feature is used. Google's handling of information is subject to its own settings and policies.

These services also receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request details, and may process or retain it under their own policies. The app uses HTTPS for its weather, search, and routing requests. We do not sell your personal data or use it to serve advertising.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Saved locations and settings remain in app storage until you remove them or clear the app's data. The last-known device location remains until replaced or app data is cleared. Cached forecasts are updated or replaced as the app is used; the cache can also be cleared manually. Android's backup service may back up app data if device backup is enabled, and a backup may restore data after reinstalling the app.

  • Turn off Track Me in the app or change its location permission in Android settings.
  • Remove saved places in the app. A deleted place may still appear in a cached forecast until the cache is cleared.
  • Use Settings → Data & Cache → Clear cache to remove stored weather forecasts. This does not remove all app settings or the last-known location.
  • Use Android's app settings to clear HluWeatherApp's storage, or uninstall the app, to remove its local data. Check your Android or Google backup settings separately if you also want to manage or delete backed-up data.

Clearing app data does not delete information already received by third-party services. Their retention and deletion practices are governed by their own policies. Android's app sandbox and HTTPS connections help protect data, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

This website and contacting us

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive your IP address and other request information when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website itself does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information in your message and use it to respond. Please avoid sending precise location or other sensitive information unless it is necessary. You may ask us to delete your correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and questions

You can choose a weather or place-search provider in the app, manage location and notification permissions in Android settings, and use the deletion options above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights concerning your personal data. For privacy questions or requests, email playstore@droopia.net.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
